About

I am a passionate Library and Information Science professional and Digital Strategist currently serving as Librarian & Social Media and Public Relations at T S Srinivasan Centre for Polytechnic and Advanced Training (CPAT-TVS), Chennai. I oversee comprehensive library and information resources catering to industry professionals, faculty, and students, leading library automation, digital preservation, and institutional knowledge infrastructures.

In an additional capacity as Digital Strategist and Social Media Manager, I steer institutional digital communications, web documentation, and public engagement. I also serve as Coordinator for the Centre for Creativity and Innovation (CCI), driving student talent discovery drives, interdisciplinary innovation projects, and active industry-academia collaborations.
